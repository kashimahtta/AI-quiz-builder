QUIZLOOM DEMO QUIZ
Topic: Photosynthesis

Use this text document as a sample question set for viewers.

QUESTION 1
Which statement best describes the main purpose of photosynthesis?

A. It converts light energy into chemical energy stored in sugars.
B. It converts oxygen into nitrogen for the atmosphere.
C. It breaks down sugars to release energy for cells.
D. It converts carbon dioxide directly into oxygen without using energy.

Answer: A
Explanation: Photosynthesis captures light energy and stores it as chemical energy in organic molecules such as sugars.

QUESTION 2
What is released when water is split during the light-dependent reactions?

A. Carbon dioxide
B. Oxygen
C. Glucose
D. Nitrogen

Answer: B
Explanation: Water is split during the light-dependent reactions, and oxygen is released as a byproduct.

QUESTION 3
What is the main role of the Calvin cycle?

A. To absorb green light using chlorophyll
B. To split water and release oxygen
C. To use carbon dioxide, ATP, and NADPH to help form sugars
D. To transport oxygen through the plant stem

Answer: C
Explanation: The Calvin cycle uses carbon dioxide and energy carriers made in the light-dependent reactions to build carbohydrates.

QUESTION 4
Why do most plants appear green?

A. Chlorophyll reflects green light more than it absorbs it.
B. Plants produce green oxygen during respiration.
C. Green light is converted directly into glucose.
D. The Calvin cycle creates a green pigment in the roots.

Answer: A
Explanation: Chlorophyll absorbs mostly red and blue light and reflects more green light, which is why leaves appear green.

QUESTION 5
Which organisms can perform photosynthesis?

A. Only flowering plants
B. Plants, algae, and cyanobacteria
C. Only animals that eat plants
D. Only fungi and bacteria that cause disease

Answer: B
Explanation: Photosynthesis is performed by plants, algae, cyanobacteria, and some other photosynthetic organisms.

QUESTION 6
Why were cyanobacteria important in Earth's history?

A. They removed all carbon dioxide from the oceans.
B. They produced oxygen that contributed to the oxygenation of Earth's atmosphere.
C. They stopped all forms of cellular respiration.
D. They converted sunlight into nitrogen gas.

Answer: B
Explanation: Oxygen-producing cyanobacteria helped increase atmospheric oxygen, making more complex life possible.
